# Clear turn rules

Players act in seat order: the host first, then each other player. Choose exactly one action on your turn, then wait for the next round. A 45-second timeout becomes a pass.

Actions: build a Farm, Tower, or Monument; gather 2 food or energy; repair the shared Core; pass; offer or accept a 1-for-1 trade; or set one shared law for the next round. Offering a trade uses your action. The recipient decides on their own turn, and accepting uses their action. Choosing another action declines the offer.

Balanced gives normal income and costs the Core 8 health. Harvest adds 1 food per Farm and costs the Core 10. Power adds 1 energy per Tower and costs the Core 10. Setting a law uses your action; later players may change it before the round ends. The last setting applies next round.

After eight rounds, the highest score wins if the Core survives.
